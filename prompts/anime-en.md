# Anime / Cel Shading

Append this block to the [common contract](en.md).

```text
[Style: Anime / Cel Shading]
- Use clean 2D anime contours, stable expressive facial features, flat base colors, and simple two-tone cel shadows on the character.
- Keep the lighting direction and shadow shapes consistent between poses. Avoid glossy highlights, elaborate hair or fabric detail, and cinematic lighting; smooth edges are welcome.
```
