# Anime Style Prompt

An anime look with cel shading, clean tapered lines, and layered eye highlights. Send this block after the shared Waddly sprite-sheet prompt.

```text
[Art style]
- Anime style with cel shading: flat base colors and hard-edged shadow shapes.
- Use clean, tapered line work that narrows toward the ends.
- Paint the eyes with layered highlights and a clear sparkle.
- Keep the palette restrained and cohesive; avoid noisy texture.
- Keep the silhouette and expression readable at small size.
```
