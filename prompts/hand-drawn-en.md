# Hand-Drawn Style Prompt

A hand-drawn look with organic, slightly wobbly lines and flat color fills. Send this block after the shared Waddly sprite-sheet prompt.

```text
[Art style]
- Hand-drawn illustration with organic lines that wobble slightly.
- Fill inside the lines with flat colors and only minimal shading.
- Keep a clean, sticker-like finish; do not make it a rough sketch or add heavy texture.
- Keep the outline and silhouette clear so the character stays readable.
- Aim for a warm, friendly feel.
```
