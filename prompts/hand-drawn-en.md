# Hand Drawn / Line Art

Append this block to the [common contract](en.md).

```text
[Style: Hand Drawn / Line Art]
- Use warm, rounded, slightly imperfect organic outlines, flat colors, simple facial features, and minimal shading, like a playful hand-drawn sticker.
- Keep line weight and contour shapes consistent between cells; do not redraw texture or add random line wobble between held poses. Smooth line edges are welcome.
```
