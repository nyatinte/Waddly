# Retro Cartoon

Append this block to the [common contract](en.md).

```text
[Style: Retro Cartoon]
- Use a playful vintage 2D cartoon look with bold rounded ink contours, expressive brows and eyes, and a restrained flat-color palette.
- Keep contour thickness and body proportions fixed. Express reactions through the face and hands, not rubbery body stretching; avoid film grain and flickering texture.
```
