# Retro Cartoon Style Prompt

A classic retro-cartoon look with thick ink lines, rounded features, and a muted palette. Send this block after the shared Waddly sprite-sheet prompt.

```text
[Art style]
- Retro-cartoon style with thick ink outlines and rounded shapes.
- Give the face a rounded beak or muzzle and pie-cut eyes.
- Draw hands and feet with the simple, rounded look of classic cartoon gloves.
- Use a muted, vintage palette and keep shading to a minimum.
- Keep the expression and features readable at small size.
```
