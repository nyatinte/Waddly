# Paper Cutout

Append this block to the [common contract](en.md).

```text
[Style: Paper Cutout]
- Build the character from layered flat colored-paper shapes with crisp cut edges and a simple, fixed silhouette.
- Keep the layer boundaries and colors identical between poses. Suggest paper through shape overlap, not cast shadows, photographic paper grain, changing folds, or 3D volume.
```
