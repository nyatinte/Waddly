# Pixel Art Style Prompt

A 16-bit-era pixel art look with a limited palette and hard edges. Send this block after the shared Waddly sprite-sheet prompt.

```text
[Art style]
- Pixel art in the style of a 16-bit-era game, with a small, cohesive palette.
- Draw hard pixel edges and crisp outlines; do not soften or blend the edges.
- Do not use anti-aliasing, blur, gradients, glow, or photorealistic rendering.
- Shade with two or three flat tones at most.
- Keep shapes, features, and expressions readable at small size.
```
