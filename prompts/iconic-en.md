# Iconic / Minimal

Append this block to the [common contract](en.md).

```text
[Style: Iconic / Minimal]
- Use a bold, recognizable mascot silhouette, a few simple geometric shapes, a small flat-color palette, and consistent stroke weight.
- Prioritize readability at small sizes. Keep facial marks and contour geometry stable, with no decorative texture or detailed shading; smooth edges are welcome.
```
